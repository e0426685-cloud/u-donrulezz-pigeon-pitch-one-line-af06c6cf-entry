## The pitch

> Coo-founder of Crumbly here: our delivery fleet already owns every statue in town, runs on breadcrumbs, and never asks for equity — just seed money, literally.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the one-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is 159 characters, a single line, in English.
